Terms of use

Effective October 19, 2026.

dms-integration is a personal desktop tool maintained by Maksim Khadkevich. It connects the owner's Dank Todo widget to the owner's Google Tasks account. No hosted synchronization service or public account registration is offered.

The owner controls installation, Google authorization, the selected task list, and whether synchronization runs. Two-way synchronization can edit or delete tasks on both sides. Keep backups of important data.

The integration is provided as-is, without guarantees of availability, uninterrupted synchronization, or recovery of deleted data. It may be changed or discontinued. Google's services remain subject to Google's applicable terms.

Stop the desktop synchronization service and revoke access in Google Account connections to disconnect it. See the privacy policy for stored-data removal details.

For technical questions, use the project issue tracker.
